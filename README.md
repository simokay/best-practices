## How to Use

Each document is structured to cover:
- **First principles** from the authoritative sources — the *why*, not just the *what*
- **Frameworks and mental models** used by practitioners
- **Checklists and practical patterns** for applying the concepts
- **Anti-patterns** — what to avoid and why
- **Key references** for deeper study
